## Current position

Tagged `v1-causal-bodily-self-report`, then extended through Probe79. The
repository contains a six-signal request protocol (need x portion size) plus an
online RLS body calibrator that learns individual bodily constants from
intermittent evidence. It remains a small structured research organism, not a
generative language agent.

Probe75 independently confirms the persistent-memory result on fresh bodies
and episode streams: **0.2100 → 0.2457 survival**, **+0.0357
[+0.0031, +0.0683], 5/5 blocks**, with all six original gates unchanged and
passing. Wrong-identity memory survives 0.2157; true rates 0.2471. A separate
standard-library checker reconstructs every gate from raw records or the
committed block sums. All blocks still share one frozen lexical/motor parent.

Probe76 then addresses the stalled structure question through causal overlap,
recovering the reachable count/partition in 25/25 K1–K5 worlds and predicting
750/750 held-out overlaps. All six gates pass, including shuffled sensory
pairing, relabeling and physical remapping. Matched forks and absent shocks are
explicit privileges; unreachable total dimension remains unresolved. Probe77
then removes those privileges, but uniform random sequential exploration fails
all five gates at both locked evidence budgets.

Probe78 establishes the value of correctly timed forgetting after hidden body
changes: oracle resets add 18 survivors in 700 episodes. Probe79 reduces
matched-history error with an observable trigger, but fails stationary
false-alarm and sign-scrambled control gates. Selective autonomous adaptation
remains unresolved; no detector survival treatment is licensed.

The preceding frontier is persistent individual evidence across episodic resets.
Probe74 holds all four metabolic constants fixed for an identity while
independently redrawing episode randomness. Current-state and uptake beliefs
reset; only a separate rate estimator's sufficient statistics persist. Five
paired blocks x 28 identities x five test episodes give 700 episodes per arm,
after one shared calibration episode per identity. Retained rates improve
survival from **0.2271 to 0.2571**, **+0.0300 [+0.0141, +0.0459] on 5/5 blocks**.
Birth-rate error falls 78.7%, matched regret improves on every block, and memory
initialized from another body loses the survival benefit. All six locked
continuation clauses pass. This is a survey that licenses independent
replication; the aggregate retained/true-rate tie does not establish saturation.
The retained evidence budget is up to 2,400 ticks against 400 for episode reset,
with matched per-tick estimator compute.

Probe73 supplies the preceding learned physical result. It runs 700 lives per
arm past Probe65's regret crossover. Every cell uses
the same recursive calibrator, filtered state, uptake belief, readings, frozen
parent and planner compute; only the request rule's rate vector is species,
learned or true. Learned rates survive **+0.0143 [+0.0003, +0.0283]** better
than the species-rate lesion, with 4/5 positive blocks and one tie. True rates
buy +0.0386 [+0.0213, +0.0559] on 5/5. Learned rates cut per-tick rate error by
81.7%, improve matched regret on every block, and change 27.5% of need words.
All five locked gates pass, although the primary lower bound is narrow.

Probe72 remains the preceding endpoint negative with a positive ceiling inside
it. True individual rates buy +0.0214 survival in its fine ecology, but the
finer quantum does not amplify that value: the paired interaction is -0.0229
[-0.0869, +0.0412], only 2/5 blocks positive. Smaller, more frequent help is
therefore closed as a survival-value amplifier even though true rates matter.

Probe71 remains the preceding construction negative. It built a default-off
five-axis body and a parameter-free first-use composition law, but its natural
range and axis-gap movement miss the locked continuation gates, so no expanded
parent or speaker treatment is built.

Probe70 remains the preceding negative: oracle-rate lifetime quotas lose
10.5–20.5 survival points at every finite store, on all 30 paired seed cells.
Correct rate labels beat species and permuted controls, but fixed quotas discard
the better realised-demand signal in the live request stream.

Two earlier findings still set the route. Probe68 gives the word-decision margin
as a measurable ecology law; Probe72 shows that law is not a sufficient account
of survival value. Probe69 shows that the structured architecture's advantage
is sample efficiency, not a ceiling: a black-box self-belief reaches the same
0.935 accuracy at roughly 64x the compute. Probe75 now completes the required
disjoint-band replication. Probe76 tests hidden restorative groups through
saturation interactions, explicitly using matched forks and no shocks, and now
passes all six gates. It addresses the stalled structure question without
reopening Probe61's latent pruning or Probe71's failed composition route. See
`decisions/2026-09-30-saturation-structure-result.md` and `STATE.md` for
the exact boundary. No evidence-budget sweep or identity-recognition mechanism
has been tested.

It remains far short of the grand vision. Bodily structure is largely stipulated,
much of the listener structure is supplied, the decision rule is
designer-supplied, utterances are selected rather than generated, the vocabulary
is three need words plus two size words, and there is no reflective or narrative
learner. Probes73–75 advance load-bearing across uses, including retained
individual evidence, but do not finish it: the frozen motor policy still does
not read the self-model, memory assignment to identities is external, and all
blocks share one parent.
