## Learned social-consequence planner

The planner enumerates all 60 tokens. For each it composes the learned
token-to-visible-help distribution with learned surface-conditioned bodily
uptake and learned depletion, then chooses the token maximizing predicted
future minimum self-belief. The implementation contains no effective-token
list or fixed need-word mapping. Need-word mappings occur only in audit scoring
and fixed/oracle controls.

## Claim boundary

This is the repository's first positive falsifiable causal self-report result:
a single persistent learned organism acquires public word semantics, learns a
causal model of its own hidden body and of how its utterances change another
agent's help, uses those models to select full-vocabulary utterances, and
maintains truthful reports under lesions, listener interventions, transfer,
and perceptible bodily counterfactuals.

It is not evidence of phenomenal consciousness, sentience, unrestricted
natural-language reflection, human-like autobiographical identity, or an
ontologically privileged `self`. The world is deterministic, the learned
state has three declared bodily axes, developmental body supervision is
available, and the result is one seed in one minimal ecology. The planner is a
structured causal algorithm rather than the failed unconstrained neural mouth.

Before any scale request, replicate the complete probe across independent
seeds and altered metabolic/help regimes, test online adaptation after causal
parameter changes, and require the same intervention pattern. No external
corpus or generated data is presently needed.
