# Proposed next roadmap after Probes77–79

Date: 2026-10-02. Status: **proposal for user approval**. This is not an
experiment preregistration and authorizes no new run by itself.

## Executive assessment

The research is moving toward the stated goal, but the complete goal remains
unearned. Learned individual rates and persistent memory demonstrably improve
survival. The latest tranche shows that obsolete memory can become costly and
correctly timed forgetting helps. However, the tested autonomous detector
fails, and structure discovery still requires laboratory privileges. The
motor policy does not consume the self-model, language remains a restricted
protocol, and causal-stage replications share one developmental parent.

The next investment should close one of those gaps with a falsifiable result.
Prioritize detecting obsolete evidence: Probe78 supplies a positive physical
instrument, so this has a clearer route to progress than expanding vocabulary
or combining unfinished mechanisms. Keep sequential structure discovery as a
separate feasibility problem. Do not tune either failed candidate into a pass.

## Completed tranche

| question | evidence | decision |
|---|---|---|
| Can random sequential actions replace matched forks for discovering groups? | Probe77 fails all five gates at one and six episodes | Close this exploration/estimator combination |
| Does timely forgetting help after hidden body changes? | Probe78: 149 to 167 survivors / 700, paired improvement +0.0257 [0.0026, 0.0488] | Timing information has physical value |
| Do repeated large prediction errors supply that timing selectively? | Probe79 improves MAE/regret but resets 17.14% of stable identities and does not reliably beat scrambled signs | Close this detector before survival treatment |

## Recommended next approval: milestones 1–2 only

| order | proposed work | deliverable and continuation condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design a distinct change-evidence model that accounts for reading gaps, ordinary shocks and estimation uncertainty | An identifiability argument and preregistration; separate stationary calibration from fresh held-out scoring; fix evidence and false-alarm budgets before evaluating |
| 2 | Run one bounded matched-history screen, then a survival treatment only if every screen gate passes | Better rate estimates and requests with controlled stationary false alarms; require evidence beyond generic resetting, using muted and reset-frequency-matched timing controls; if passed, separately preregister physical survival benefit and stable-body preservation |
| 3 | Develop a directed sequential structure experiment | First show why its proposed interventions can identify action groups within lived evidence budgets; preserve opaque IDs, K1–K5, shocks, remapping and causal pairing controls; stop if the feasibility argument fails |
| 4 | Integrate established capabilities and replicate development | Give the same model a causal role in movement, prediction and requests; lesion each use; repeat across independently developed parents before broad claims |
| 5 | Expand communication when the task demands new meanings | Introduce a listener decision that needs uncertainty or a novel combination; test unseen demands and destructive controls, rather than counting vocabulary size |

Milestone 1 does not mean interpreting existing RLS covariance as calibrated
uncertainty: it shrinks under stationary assumptions and cannot itself detect
staleness. A candidate could compare explanations of new readings under
continued old dynamics versus changed dynamics, but its observable inputs,
calibration protocol and assumptions must be explicit before implementation.
If that argument is inadequate, stop at the design review.

Milestone 2 must use fresh evaluation streams. Probe79 is development evidence,
not a reusable test set. A reset-frequency-matched timing control would ask
whether timing adds value beyond merely discarding memory; its construction
must avoid future information reaching the learner. Correctly timed oracle
reset remains a reference instrument, not a universal upper bound.

The proposed scope is one new mechanism and one fixed screen, with the
closed-loop treatment conditional on all screen gates. A failure produces a
result document and a stop, not another parameter sweep. No larger model,
external corpus, new sensor, or legacy training rewrite is included.

## Measuring progress and effort

Track claims earned: selective correction, physical benefit, structure without
forks, a shared model across uses, productive communication, and replication
across parents. Passing software tests and adding probes are supporting work,
not substitutes for those claims.

Do not assign a completion percentage to the full goal. The remaining gaps
require discoveries, so a calendar promise would be misleading. For planning
compute, this tranche's full screens took about 24 seconds (Probe77), 36.6
minutes (Probe78) and 5.5 minutes (Probe79). A new mechanism's run estimate must
come from its own diagnostic before launching. Design and validation, rather
than raw compute, are likely to dominate the next milestone.

After approval, begin with milestone 1. Milestones 3–5 describe the longer
roadmap and remain outside the next bounded tranche.
